Country Coincidence Same Oppose Partial Absent Change (2013-23)

"Coincidence" measures the percentage of votes where a country voted the same as the United States, calculated as (Same + 0.5 * Partial) / (Same + Oppose + Partial). Data from US State Department "Voting Practices in the United Nations" annual reports. The sharp jump from 2016 to 2018 reflects a methodological change in how votes were counted, not purely a political shift.
